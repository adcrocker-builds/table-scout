Why We Like It
Travel to three distinct regions (Italy, Lebanon, and Spain) in one night at this lively Georgetown spot. The room — earthy terracotta-colored walls and prints galore — sets the stage for a tour around the Mediterranean. Definitely take a group: You’ll want to share everything, from burrata and caviar to wagyu beef carpaccio, and from lobster gnocchi diavolo to the slow smoked lamb shoulder.
